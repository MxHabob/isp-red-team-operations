# Glossary

- **ROE** — Rules of Engagement.
- **TTP** — Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures.
- **PCAP** — Packet capture.
- **Evidence manifest** — metadata describing evidence and its integrity.
- **Baseline** — controlled measurements taken before a scenario.
- **Finding** — validated observation with security/business significance.
- **Retest** — controlled verification after remediation.
- **Kill switch** — mechanism/process for immediately stopping an operation.
