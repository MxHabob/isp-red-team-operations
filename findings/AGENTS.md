# Findings — Validated Security Observations

## Responsibility
Finding records, severity assessments, remediation tracking, and retest results.

## Rules
- A finding must have an observation supported by evidence.
- Do not convert a hypothesis to a finding without evidence.
- Distinguish observation, analysis, impact, confidence, and limitations.
- Finding IDs must be stable once referenced by reports.
- Store finding records as JSON in `records/`.
