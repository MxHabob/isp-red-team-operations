# Research — Vulnerability Research and Hypothesis Management

## Responsibility
Manage testable hypotheses about ISP security controls, attack surface analysis, protocol analysis, and policy analysis.

## Rules
- Every hypothesis must be independently testable within authorized scope.
- Do not assume results — hypotheses are questions, not conclusions.
- Record negative results (controls working correctly) as well as positive results.
- Do not build bypass tools. Build test harnesses that can prove or disprove hypotheses.
- Keep hypothesis IDs stable once referenced by scenarios or findings.
